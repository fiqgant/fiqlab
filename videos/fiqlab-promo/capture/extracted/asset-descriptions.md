# Asset inventory

No binary assets were captured. This is a **no-capture** build: the brief
declares the design system authoritative from the fiqlab.dev source repo, and
the terminal aesthetic is recreated in-composition (pure CSS/HTML — black
ground, cyan glow text, CRT scanlines, blinking cursor). No logos, screenshots,
photos, or data files are needed.

All on-screen content (typed commands, headings, blog titles, stats, URL) is
text rendered in JetBrains Mono per `tokens.json` / `visible-text.txt`.
